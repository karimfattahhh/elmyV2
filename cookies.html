01

What cookies are

Cookies are small values stored by a browser on behalf of a website. Similar technologies can include local storage, tracking pixels and other mechanisms that store information on, or read information from, a device. Browser caching of files and ordinary network requests are related technical activities but are not the same as an advertising cookie.

02

Current website inventory

Reviewed on 22 September 2026: the first-party website code contains no cookie-setting calls, no localStorage or sessionStorage writes, and no analytics or advertising tags. No site-defined cookie names, purposes or expiry periods therefore appear in the current inventory.

The website’s navigation and search run locally in your browser after loading a small search index from this site. Contact-form draft values are held temporarily in page memory; the preview does not transmit them. Our code does not intentionally persist these values across visits.

This source-code inventory does not certify the behavior of a future production host, CDN, security service or added integration. A browser and response-header audit of the final deployment must confirm whether those services add cookies or similar technologies.

03

Fonts, video and partner links

The site uses external Google Fonts styles and font files. These requests disclose connection information to Google and may be cached by your browser. Their privacy implications must be reviewed independently of whether cookies are used. See the Privacy Policy for the external-provider disclosure.

The methodology video and the embedded product film are served from this website; no third-party video player was identified in the reviewed implementation. Following an external partner link loads the partner’s website, whose own policies apply.

04

Your choices

You can manage or delete cookies and stored website data through your browser settings. Browser settings can also control caching and some tracking technologies. Blocking all storage may affect other websites and future features that genuinely require it.

This version has no optional analytics or advertising categories to turn on or off, so it does not present a consent banner or a non-functional cookie-preferences switch. Closing this page or continuing to browse is not treated as consent to future optional tracking.

05

If the website adds tracking

Before optional cookies or similar technologies are introduced, assess the applicable law, update this inventory and implement any required prior consent. Where consent is needed, optional technologies should remain disabled until the visitor chooses them, with clear choices and a practical way to withdraw.

The final inventory must list each relevant technology, provider, purpose, category and duration, including third-party technologies and any claimed exemption. Essential security or delivery functions must also be documented if the production host introduces them.
