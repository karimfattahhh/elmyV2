02

Information involved

When you visit, the hosting and delivery infrastructure receives technical information needed to respond to your browser, such as IP address, requested address, browser information and request time. Whether these details are logged, and for how long, depends on the production hosting configuration, which remains to be confirmed.

The inquiry form asks for your inquiry type, name, work email, institution, institution type and message, and may ask for your role, solution interests and approximate learner count. In this review version, submission is disabled: our form code does not transmit these entries, and temporary form drafts are held in page memory rather than browser storage. Do not enter real confidential information into the preview.

Once inquiry delivery is enabled, this notice must identify the receiving system and accurately describe its operation. Information you send through other approved contact channels may include business correspondence and the details you choose to provide.

03

Purposes and proposed legal bases

For an operational website, proposed purposes are delivering pages, protecting the service, responding to inquiries, arranging demonstrations, discussing institutional requirements and maintaining necessary business records. We do not use website search queries to make automated decisions about learners; the search code processes queries in your browser.

Where a law requires a legal basis, the basis must be selected for each actual purpose. Potential bases include legitimate interests in operating a secure business website and responding to business inquiries, steps requested before a contract where applicable, compliance with legal obligations, and consent for optional activities that require it. These are proposed bases for legal review, not a claim that every basis applies in every country.

An inquiry is not a general agreement to receive marketing. Any future newsletter or promotional subscription must have an appropriate separate choice and a working way to unsubscribe.

04

Providers and external services

The current site requests font styles and files from Google Fonts. These external requests expose connection information, including your IP address, to the font provider. Your browser may also send request headers. The legal team should review this dependency and whether to serve fonts locally.

Website assets, search data and the methodology video are otherwise served from the website in this reviewed version. Links to partner websites take you to independently operated services whose own privacy notices apply.

Before production, identify the hosting, security, email, CRM, scheduling and other providers actually used, their roles, locations and contractual safeguards. Limit disclosures to authorized people and providers who need information for the stated purposes. Any disclosure required by law or connected with a business transfer must be assessed and described as appropriate.

05

Cookies and browser storage

The reviewed website code does not set cookies or write to localStorage or sessionStorage. No analytics or advertising tags were identified. This does not establish what a future hosting provider, embedded service or deployment configuration may add. See the Cookie Policy for the current inventory and the changes that require reassessment.

08

Your choices and rights

Depending on the law that applies, you may have rights to access information, correct inaccuracies, request deletion, restrict or object to processing, receive portable information, or withdraw consent for consent-based processing. Withdrawing consent does not undo processing that was lawful before withdrawal. Some rights are subject to conditions or lawful exceptions.

Send requests to [PRIVACY EMAIL]. We may need proportionate information to verify your identity and understand your request, and will apply the relevant legal response deadline. Where applicable, you may complain to the competent data-protection authority: [AUTHORITY / CONTACT TO CONFIRM].

The contact form is not currently a working rights-request channel. A monitored privacy contact must be provided before publication. Rights concerning records controlled by your institution should ordinarily be directed to that institution, with Elmy’s assistance where required by its agreement and applicable law.

09

Children and learner information

This marketing website is intended primarily for institutions, educators and other business visitors. It does not provide learner account registration. Do not include student records, children’s personal information, health information or other sensitive information in a website inquiry.

If such information is received inadvertently, its handling and deletion should be assessed promptly. Any service involving children or learner records requires a separate review of applicable education and privacy laws, institutional authorization, parental permissions where required and appropriate safeguards.

10

Security and accountability

Appropriate access controls, secure transmission, provider management and incident procedures must support the final data-handling arrangements. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure. This draft does not claim a security certification, independent security audit or compliance accreditation.

The current reviewed code contains no advertising or sale/sharing-for-advertising integration. The legal team must confirm broader organizational practices and any jurisdiction-specific disclosures before making an organization-wide representation.
