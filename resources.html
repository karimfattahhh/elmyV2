ELMY IMPACT / ACADEMIC SUPPORT

From evidence to an agreed support plan

With Elmy Impact, your academic team can review findings with Elmy’s experts, discuss recommendations and plan the agreed follow-up. Your institution retains responsibility for academic decisions.

  1. 01

    Review evidence

    Your team shares assessment findings and relevant curriculum, teaching and learner context.

  2. 02

    Interpret findings

    Elmy’s academic experts examine patterns with your team and explain what the evidence can—and cannot—establish.

  3. 03

    Recommend action

    Discuss priorities for practice, teaching adjustments or further investigation. Your team decides what to implement.

  4. 04

    Review follow-up

    At agreed review points, examine subsequent evidence and discuss whether the support approach needs to change.
