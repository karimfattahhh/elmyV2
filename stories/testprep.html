01 / THE APPROACH

Review diagnostic results

Assessment results identify demonstrated skills and topic-level gaps. Educators use these findings to select relevant practice.

02 / THE APPROACH

Identify shared learning needs

Educators interpret the results and identify learners who need similar support. The visual separates learning needs; it does not rank students.

03 / THE APPROACH

Assign practice and review performance

The academic team assigns relevant practice, follows performance and adjusts support as understanding develops.

01 / LEARNING IN PRACTICE

The requirement

TestPrep required diagnostic evidence to differentiate practice and support by academic need.

02 / LEARNING IN PRACTICE

Elmy’s contribution

With Elmy, diagnostic performance became the starting point. Students were assessed, strengths and gaps identified, and learners grouped by academic need. Educators could see performance clearly and assign targeted practice instead of the same work for everyone.

03 / LEARNING IN PRACTICE

Operational application

Academic teams review performance and recurring errors to adjust practice and support.
