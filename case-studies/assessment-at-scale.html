The challenge

Deliver a structured assessment experience at institutional scale while coordinating testing logistics, technology, student management and timely reporting.

02 / OPERATIONS

The approach

Elmy supported the assessment infrastructure behind the initiative, bringing digital testing, student management, technology and reporting into one coordinated process.

03 / OPERATIONS

Preparing for delivery

Before implementation, the team ran a full operational and technology dry run: devices, connectivity, room setup, student flows, communication procedures and contingency plans.

The program brought digital testing, candidate management and reporting into a coordinated assessment process. The experience reinforced the importance of rehearsing both the technology and the on-site operation before launch.
