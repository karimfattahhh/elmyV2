Reporting and academic support

Assessment reporting and academic review

Review topic performance, response timing and question difficulty. Educators interpret the evidence; Elmy Impact adds academic consultation within the agreed scope.

The resultWhat educators can investigate

Topic-level evidence helps an educator identify where to look more closely and select relevant practice. A broad score alone cannot make that decision.

Response timing and answer changes provide context. An educator reviews them alongside academic evidence before drawing a conclusion.

Averages can hide different performance patterns. Educators can review difficulty and question-level results before deciding where practice should focus.

Completion and activity signals can prompt a closer look. The educator checks the learner’s context and decides whether support is needed.
